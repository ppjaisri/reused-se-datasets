Reused datasets in empirical SE research

The datasets that empirical studies in ten software engineering venues (ASE, FSE, ICSE, ICSME, MSR, SANER, EMSE, JSS, TOSEM, TSE) reused instead of, or in addition to, collecting their own data. Each dataset lists the papers that reuse it.

What do these fields mean?
Search
Finds datasets whose name, other names used in the papers, first author, or cited reference contain all the words you type.
Topic
The research topic of the reusing paper: an ACM CCS 2012 level-2 concept under Software and its engineering. Shows datasets reused by at least one paper on that topic.
Venue
The conference or journal where the reusing paper was published.
Paper year
The publication year of the reusing paper (the journal volume or conference edition), not the year of the dataset.
Source type
Where the reusing paper's data come from. Existing dataset: the paper uses only datasets built by earlier work. Hybrid: the paper combines existing datasets with data it collects itself.
Reused by
The minimum number of papers that reuse the dataset, counted after the other filters are applied.
Year (column)
The publication year of the dataset as cited by the reusing papers. A dash means the year could not be determined.
Papers (column)
The number of papers that reuse the dataset. With filters on, it shows matching papers / all papers.

About the data

  • A use is one paper reusing one dataset. The 184 papers that reuse data make 426 uses of 313 distinct datasets.
  • Year is the publication year of the dataset as cited by the reusing papers. Twelve datasets have no year that could be determined.
  • Topics are the ACM CCS 2012 level-2 concepts under Software and its engineering assigned to the reusing papers.
  • Own dataset marks a paper whose first author is also the first author of the dataset.
  • Dataset names are those used in this study; the names used in the papers are listed under Also called.
